# Proving costs

Generated by `eid-vectors costs` (`rust/eid-vectors/src/costs.rs`) from the registry fixtures (DE and IT master lists) and the measurements in `docs/data/`. Do not edit by hand; re-measure with `scripts/measure-proving.sh` and regenerate.

## What is measured

- **Circuit:** the DSC step (step A in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md)). It parses the DSC certificate, checks the CSCA key in the registry at the DSC's `notBefore` and non-revocation, hashes the certificate and verifies the CSCA signature. The SOD and envelope steps are not built yet (see the end of this document).
- **Gates** come from `bb gates -t noir-recursive` for every generated circuit (`docs/data/circuit-sizes.json`).
- **Proving time and peak memory** come from `bb prove` on Apple M5 Max, 18 cores (6 performance + 12 efficiency), 48 GB (bb 5.0.0-nightly.20260522, nargo 1.0.0-beta.22, 2026-09-27), with 18 threads and with 4 threads, for the 31 circuits that have a real-certificate `Prover.toml`. Every proof was verified. Circuits without a measurement are estimated from gates with a least-squares fit over the measured ones (marked ≈): 4 threads `5.32 s per million gates + 0.09 s`, 18 threads `2.81 s/Mgate + 0.07 s`, memory `2309 bytes per gate -36 MiB`.
- **Phone estimates are assumptions, not measurements:** laptop time with 4 threads × 2 for a recent flagship phone, × 6 for a mid-range Android phone. Measure one circuit on a real device (bb has iOS and Android builds) and scale these columns by the observed ratio.
- **Memory** matters as much as time on phones: several circuits peak above 1 GiB. Every circuit is capped at 2 GiB of proving memory: CI fails any circuit above 858,993 gates (2 GiB at 2,500 bytes per gate; measured 1,985–2,418), and `scripts/measure-proving.sh` fails on a measured peak above 2 GiB.

## The other steps

The SOD and envelope steps and the aggregation are not built yet, so their cost is not measured. What they will contain:

- **SOD step:** reads the DSC key from the committed `TBSCertificate`, recomputes the step A commitment (Poseidon2 over the bucket), hashes the SOD signed attributes (100–200 bytes) and verifies the DSC signature. It has no large hash, so it should cost roughly the signature check plus parsing. Signature-only costs are in [noir/bench](../noir/bench/README.md): for example RSA-4096 PKCS#1 v1.5 117k gates, ECDSA P-256 95k.
- **Envelope step:** hashes DG1 (~90 bytes) and DG11 (up to a few hundred bytes), the eContent and the signed attributes' digest check, then encrypts with a Poseidon2 duplex and one Grumpkin ECDH per viewer. Grumpkin is Noir's native curve, so ECDH is cheap. The hashing dominates: SHA-256 over 2 KiB is 154k gates, SHA-1 379k.
- **Aggregation** recursively verifies the three proofs. It only sees proofs and public inputs, and the commitments are salted, so it can run off the phone without revealing document data.
