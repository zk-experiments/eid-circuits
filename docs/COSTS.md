# Proving costs

Generated by `eid-vectors costs` (`rust/eid-vectors/src/costs.rs`) from the registry fixtures (DE and IT master lists) and the measurements in `docs/data/`. Do not edit by hand; re-measure with `mise run fold:record` and regenerate.

## What is measured

- **A document proof** is one Chonk proof: the DSC, SOD and document steps folded by noir-zk's generic kernels (`kernel_init`, two `kernel_step`s, `kernel_hiding`) in the test-only pipeline `[dsc, sod, document]` ([FOLDING.md](FOLDING.md)). A real pipeline adds its envelope app and whatever else it folds, one `kernel_step` each.
- **Gates** come from `bb gates --scheme chonk` for every circuit (`docs/data/circuit-sizes.json`).
- **Proving time and peak memory** come from `mise run fold:record` (`rust/eid-circuits/tests/fold.rs`, in-process through noir-zk's `PipelineFold`) on Apple M5 Max (18 cores), macOS (bb 7.0.0-nightly.20260927, nargo 1.0.0-rc.3, 2026-09-29), with 18 threads and with 4 threads, for the synthetic documents of `noir/circuits/chains.json`. Every proof was verified. Other documents are estimated (≈) with least-squares fits: time against a document's total gates (4 threads `8.44 s per million gates + -0.92 s`, 18 threads `6.97 s/Mgate + -1.34 s`), and peak memory against its largest circuit (`2050 bytes per gate +779 MiB`), since Chonk proves one circuit at a time.
- **Per-country documents are an assumption:** the master lists hold CSCAs only, so each country's DSC is assumed to sign with its CSCA's configuration, and its LDS to use that configuration's hash in the 512-byte eContent bucket. Real DSC statistics (the ICAO PKD DSC list) would replace this.
- **Phone estimates are assumptions, not measurements:** laptop time with 4 threads × 2 for a recent flagship phone, × 6 for a mid-range Android phone. Measure a document proof on a real device and scale these columns by the observed ratio.
- **Memory cap.** Every circuit is capped at 2 GiB of proving memory: CI fails any circuit above 858,993 gates (2 GiB at 2,500 bytes per gate).
