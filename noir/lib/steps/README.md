# eid_steps

Checks shared by the step circuits in `noir/circuits`. A step circuit is a thin generated binary that hashes and verifies one signature with the library for its signature group, and calls into this library for everything else.

- **`dsc::check::<T, K, M>(root, witness, kind) -> TbsFields`** runs the DSC step checks: TBS walk, zero padding, leaf header against `kind`, registry leaf at `notBefore`, non-revocation of the serial. See [docs/circuits/dsc.md](../../../docs/circuits/dsc.md).
- **`dsc::commitment(salt, country, hash_id, tbs, len)`** is the step A output, `H(salt, country, hash_id, len, pack_be(tbs))`.
- **`sod::parse::<T, A>(tbs, attrs, md_offset) -> (TbsFields, SignedAttrs)`** runs the SOD step checks besides the signature: TBS walk and padding, signed attributes walk and padding, the messageDigest attribute. See [docs/circuits/sod.md](../../../docs/circuits/sod.md).
- **`sod::commitment(salt, country, attrs)`** is the step B output, `H(salt, country, digest_len, pack_be(digest))`.
- **`document::check::<E, O, D>(date, witness, hash_oid) -> Parsed`** runs the document step checks besides hashing: LDS walk and padding, DG1 (MRZ) parsing, country, expiry. `document::assert_message_digest` and `document::assert_hash_at` compare the hashes the circuit computes, and `document::outputs` builds the step's record: step B's commitment, DG1's payload commitment (zk-encryption's `channel::payload::commit` over `document::plaintext`), the date, the scope and `document::nullifier`. See [docs/circuits/document.md](../../../docs/circuits/document.md).
- **`country_field(country)`** packs three ICAO letters into one field, as both commitments do.
- **`HASH_SHA1` … `HASH_SHA512`** are the hash ids `dsc::commitment` commits to (the DSC step's TBS hash; no step exposes them).

Tests: `src/vectors.nr` (generated by `eid-vectors steps`) runs `dsc::check` on 33 real certificates against the fixture registry, `src/sod_vectors.nr` runs `sod::parse` and the DSC key read on five synthetic documents, and `src/document_vectors.nr` runs `document::check` on two synthetic documents and `parse_dg1` on TD1, TD2 and TD3 MRZs. `src/tests.nr`, `src/sod_tests.nr` and `src/document_tests.nr` hold the negative cases. `eid_hash` is a dependency for the tests only.
